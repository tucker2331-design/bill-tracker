New = first session in the House. Bills = bills they have filed this session. Carried = bills on these three laws they filed since 2023. A seat on the room: majority members of a PA committee got 20 points more of their own bills out than majority members off it; a majority chair's own bills got out 80% vs 41%. New members file fewer bills (median 8 vs 14 in the majority) and got theirs out at about the same rate.
Tested, and it did not hold: districts' make-up barely predicts who carries a subject. Of 16 subject-and-chamber tests, 2 held (farming in the Senate, schools in the House); about 1 would pass by chance alone. So these district numbers are your argument in the meeting, not a forecast of who says yes.
