How the page is laid out, and why
Count and shareAlways together (owner rule): the share says how much it matters to this district, the count says how many people stand behind the ask. Places are counts only.
Map + tableThe map answers "where"; the ranked table answers "how many" and is the way to read exact values. A map is never the only way to read a number.
SimulatorChoices the drafter controls (which law, whose name on it) are chips; each gate shows its own k of n. We never multiply gates into one percentage.
Carrier tableFacts per member, A–Z. A ranked "best carrier" is a prediction and needs the calibration gate and your go-ahead first.
Fact sheetOne wording for every district; only the numbers change. Margins of error shown.
ColourGrey for data; blue only for links and the selected district; amber and red only for caution and "did not hold" on this page.
