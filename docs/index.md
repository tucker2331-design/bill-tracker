- [[testing/rooms]] — **THE ROOM: who decides and how many of them. The deciding body is a SUBCOMMITTEE OF EIGHT, and 86% of its kills are strictly party-line (median 1 crosser when anyone crosses). Explains the flat majority-size relationship: an 8-seat room at 5-3 and at 6-2 vote the same, so seats past the majority buy nothing. CORRECTS the 'agenda access' reading — 83% of bills get a roll call, access is near-equal (85%/81%), and the gap among HEARD bills (+35pt) is LARGER than the raw gap (+31pt). Four nulls: time-since-referral, the substitute, the subcommittee discontinuity (fails balance), and the retracted majority-recovery claim (63 of 82 bills were one session).**
- [[testing/carrier_effect]] — **THE CARRIER vs THE CAUCUS, and the co-patron RETRACTION. Incorporation contests separate the two decisions: when the room has already decided to advance a policy and only the vehicle is open, it keeps the MAJORITY member's bill 83% of the time (4.8:1, n=208) — filing order and seniority both null. Carrier effectiveness is real and persistent (true SD 14-16pt, year-to-year r=0.46-0.51, ~half the party effect). RETRACTS the cross-party co-patron lever: Open States co-patron lists are undated snapshots, 69% of incorporation survivors absorb the loser's patron, and on LIS's pre-treatment labels the cross-party premium is +4pt (p=0.39) / +11pt (p=0.20).**
- [[testing/bill_mix_confound]] — **THE BILL-MIX CONFOUND, KILLED — and the lobbyist/legislator split. The +26pt majority swing survives holding subject (+28), the bill idea (+28) and the exact title (+31) constant; portfolios do not get easier in power (p=0.11); the effect REVERSES on losing the chamber (+29 gained / −21 lost). Separates CONTEXT (standing, subject difficulty — not levers) from LEVERS a lobbyist owns: one cross-party co-patron is +26pt for a MINORITY patron and +9 for a majority one.**

## Conventions (for the LLM maintaining this wiki)

- **Wikilinks over markdown links** where possible — Obsidian's graph view and backlinks depend on `[[name]]` syntax.
- **Section-anchored wikilinks** when pointing at a specific header in another page: `[[page#Section Header|display text]]`. Lets Obsidian jump straight to the header on click. Don't write `[[page]] → "Section"` with a manual arrow — the section anchor is the link.
- **Frontmatter on every page:** `tags`, `updated: YYYY-MM-DD`, and `status: active | archived | stub`.
- **One concept per page.** If a page covers two separate things, split it.
- **Cross-reference instead of duplicate.** If information belongs on page A, reference it from page B with a wikilink rather than copy-pasting.
- **Update on touch.** Whenever a page is read in service of a task, update the `updated:` field if the content needs refreshing.
- **New lessons → new pages.** Each post-mortem or framework insight gets its own page in `failures/`, then a link from the index.
